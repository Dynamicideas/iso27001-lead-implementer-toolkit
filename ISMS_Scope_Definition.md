# Information Security Management System (ISMS) Scope Statement
**Standard Alignment:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.3 (Determining the Scope of the ISMS)

## 1. Boundary & Applicability
This ISMS encompasses the people, processes, and technology infrastructure powering the organization’s core **Production Cloud Architecture and E-Commerce API platforms**. 

### Included Boundaries:
* All AWS cloud accounts holding customer data.
* Corporate Identity Providers (Okta/Active Directory) controlling logical employee access.
* The physical corporate headquarters infrastructure located in Berlin, Germany.

### Excluded Boundaries:
* Third-party vendor-managed offices and software systems (managed under supplier control matrix *A.5.19*).

## 2. Strategic Objectives
* Attain a **zero-major-non-conformity** status during the upcoming Stage 2 external certification assessment.
* Ensure a 100% completion rate for mandatory security awareness training cycles across all internal business units.
